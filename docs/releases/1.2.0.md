Opening a torrent file or magnet link brings Sweep’s existing library window forward. If you open several files, Sweep presents one Add dialog at a time. Closing the library window lets transfers continue in the background.

You can now route torrent traffic directly over Wi-Fi while other apps use your VPN. Enable **Use direct Wi-Fi for torrents** in **Sweep → Settings → Network**, approve the helper when macOS prompts you, and relaunch Sweep. Trackers and peers will see your Wi-Fi connection’s public IP.

Your saved torrents appear as soon as Sweep opens. The status bar shows when direct Wi-Fi is connecting. If the connection fails, transfers wait and you can retry from the status bar.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. To update from Sweep 1.1.0, choose **Sweep → Check for Updates…**.
