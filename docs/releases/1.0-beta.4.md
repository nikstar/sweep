Sweep 1.0 (4) — private iOS TestFlight beta

- Magnet metadata discovery retries automatically after timeouts, with visible
  attempt counts and retry delays. Pause and remove cancel pending retries.
- DHT contacts persist across relaunches. Network health separates cached
  contacts, bootstrap resolution, queries, replies, and timeouts.
- Peer Exchange helps find more peers while resolving magnet metadata.
- MSE/PE peer connections and TCP/uTP fallback improve interoperability with
  clients that require encrypted connections.
- Peer and tracker diagnostics show where discovery or connection setup fails.
- Live Activities use a translucent background and end when a transfer pauses
  or fails. Background completion remains briefly visible until reopening Sweep.
- Partially downloaded files can open in place, and growing media can reload.

Please test magnet discovery on your usual connection, pause/resume during a
metadata retry, and restoration after relaunch. In Health and the torrent's
Activity/Peers pages, check whether failures happen during discovery, connection,
handshake, or metadata transfer. Test Live Activity dismissal and opening a video
while it is still downloading.

Some networks or VPN configurations still block peer or DHT traffic. BEP 55
hole punching is not implemented; these changes do not guarantee that every
swarm is reachable.
