Sweep 1.0 is the first stable macOS release. It runs on **macOS 15 or later**
on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Download **Sweep-1.0-5-macOS-universal.zip**, unzip it, and drag **Sweep.app**
to **Applications**. The app is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

- Open a magnet link or a `.torrent` file, choose which files to download, and
  resume your transfers when you reopen Sweep.
- Use the Files inspector to select just the videos in a torrent, open a file
  in its default app, or reveal it in Finder.
- Inspect trackers, peers, and metadata discovery when a transfer stalls.
  Sweep retries magnet discovery after timeouts and keeps looking for DHT
  peers when connectivity returns after a long outage.
- Connect to peers over TCP or uTP, including peers that require BitTorrent
  protocol encryption.
