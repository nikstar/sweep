Sweep now asks before removing an unfinished torrent from your library, including when you press Delete. Removing an entry keeps its downloaded files. Press **⌘Z** to bring the entry back. The separate trash button asks before permanently deleting files.

The Mac toolbar puts Add, Resume/Pause, and Remove together on the left. The circled minus removes a library entry, and the Inspector sits at the far right.

Direct Wi-Fi now recovers from stale helper registrations after an update. If macOS still can’t start the helper, the status bar offers **Retry** and transfers wait for the direct interface.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. To update, choose **Sweep → Check for Updates…**.
